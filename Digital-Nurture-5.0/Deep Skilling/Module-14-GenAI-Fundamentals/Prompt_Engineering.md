# Prompt Engineering

## What is Prompt Engineering?

Prompt Engineering is writing effective instructions for AI.

---

## Types of Prompts

### Zero Shot

Write a C# program to reverse a string.

---

### Few Shot

Input

abc

Output

cba

Now reverse "hello".

---

### Chain of Thought

Explain step by step how Binary Search works.

---

## Best Practices

- Give clear instructions
- Provide context
- Mention output format
- Keep prompts simple
- Improve prompts iteratively

---

## Ethics

- Avoid biased prompts
- Protect privacy
- Verify AI responses
