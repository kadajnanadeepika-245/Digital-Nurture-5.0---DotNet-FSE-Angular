# Scrum Framework

## Scrum Roles

### Product Owner

- Manages Product Backlog
- Prioritizes Features

---

### Scrum Master

- Facilitates Scrum
- Removes Impediments

---

### Development Team

- Develops Product
- Delivers Increment

---

## Scrum Ceremonies

- Sprint Planning
- Daily Scrum
- Sprint Review
- Sprint Retrospective

---

## Scrum Artifacts

- Product Backlog
- Sprint Backlog
- Increment

---

## Definition of Done

The feature is complete, tested, reviewed, and ready for release.
