# Agile Estimation

## Story Points

Story points estimate effort instead of time.

Example

Small = 1

Medium = 3

Large = 5

Extra Large = 8

---

## Planning Poker

Each team member selects a story point card.

Discussion continues until everyone agrees.

---

## Velocity

Velocity = Total Story Points Completed in Sprint

---

## Burndown Chart

Shows remaining work against sprint duration.
